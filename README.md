# Sourav Prasad

PhD Student in Finance and Accounting at the **Indian Institute of Management Bodh Gaya**

I study how investor behaviour and sustainability shape financial markets. My work sits at the intersection of behavioral finance, ESG, and climate finance, and I aim to produce research that is rigorous, policy relevant, and useful to practitioners.

## Research Interests

- **Behavioral finance**: investor sentiment, attention, and its effect on asset prices
- **Sustainability and ESG**: disclosure, ratings, and firm value
- **Climate finance**: green bonds, climate risk pricing, and transition finance
- **Corporate finance and accounting**: reporting quality and market reactions

My long term goal is to contribute to leading finance and accounting journals (FT50 and UTD 24).

## Writing and Outreach

- **Green Finance Insights**: a weekly LinkedIn newsletter on climate and green finance, read by more than 1,100 subscribers
- Regular posts on finance, accounting, and sustainability for a LinkedIn community of more than 6,600 followers
- Contributor to LinkedIn Collaborative Articles on finance and climate topics
